# Privacy Policy – Save Image As (PNG, JPG, WebP)

_Last updated: 2026-11-23_

This extension does not collect, store, sell or transmit any personal data.

- Images you choose to save are downloaded from the website where they are hosted,
  converted on your own device, and saved to your computer. They are never sent to us or any third party.
- The extension makes no network requests of its own: no analytics, no tracking, no ads, no remote code.
- Site access permissions are requested only when you first save an image from a site, and are used only
  to read the image you selected.
- You can revoke site access at any time from `chrome://extensions` → this extension → Details → Site access.

Source code: https://github.com/KimDongHwan/save-image-as
